Award-Winning Projects

How a Homegrown Company is Setting New Standards with Award-Winning Construction Projects in Rwanda.

What began as a bold idea in 2013 has grown into one of Rwanda’s most respected construction material companies. TEMACO Builders Ltd, founded by Chemistry graduate Emmanuel Tuyisenge, started with a clear mission: to deliver durable, aesthetic, and innovative paving and cladding solutions. Through resilience, hands-on leadership, and an unshakable commitment to quality, TEMACO quickly rose from a startup to a recognized industry player. Today, its projects stand as award-worthy examples of how homegrown talent, trust, and vision can transform the built environment and set new standards across the country.

Winning the Award for Inclusive and Sustainable Sanitation Solutions

TEMACO Builders received national recognition for its groundbreaking work on the EcoTeto toilet system—an eco-friendly, durable sanitation solution designed with inclusivity at its core. This award celebrated TEMACO’s commitment to public health, sustainability, and innovation in construction. What set the EcoTeto apart was its thoughtful design: child-sized toilet seats and accessible features tailored for people with disabilities, ensuring dignity and safety for all users. The project not only elevated sanitation standards but also showcased how construction can address real social challenges through smart, empathetic design. This milestone reinforced TEMACO’s role as a forward-thinking leader in Rwanda’s construction sector.

Award-Winning Excellence and Lasting Impact

TEMACO Builders Ltd has distinguished itself through projects recognized for their innovation, quality, and social impact. With a commitment to craftsmanship and sustainability, the company has earned prestigious awards for designs such as the inclusive EcoTeto toilets—featuring seats for children and people with disabilities. These accolades reflect TEMACO’s dedication not only to superior construction but also to addressing community needs and promoting accessibility. Through these award-winning projects, TEMACO continues to raise the bar for Rwanda’s construction industry, proving that excellence and social responsibility go hand in hand.
