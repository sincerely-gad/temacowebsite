Our Products

High-quality concrete construction materials manufactured with precision and care to meet all your building needs.

Floor Tiles

Floor Tiles

Pavers

Pavers

Wide range of concrete pavers with attractive textures and colors to enhance outdoor spaces while providing terrain protection and value addition.

Cement Blocks

Cement Blocks

Concrete masonry units (CMU) for building material in wall construction, manufactured in different types for building beauty and safety.

Cast Stones

Cast stones

Highly refined architectural precast concrete stones as alternatives to natural stones that are hard to find, manage, or expensive.

Cladding

Cladding

Premium cladding for superior weather protection and insulation, enhancing any building's appearance.

Hollow Bricks

Hollow Bricks

Lightweight and durable hollow concrete blocks designed for efficient construction, providing excellent thermal insulation and structural stability.

Kerbs

Kerbs

Garden and landscape edging solutions to redefine spaces, facilitate maintenance, and enhance the appearance of landscaped areas.

CEB Blocks

CEB Blocks

Compressed Earth Blocks made from compressed soil and cement, perfect for foundations, load-bearing walls, arches, and vaults.

Terrazzo

Terrazzo

Grass Block Pavers

Permeable pavers that allow grass growth while providing flood protection, preserving lawn aesthetics with eco-friendly appeal.

Our Services

From concept to completion — TEMACO delivers fully integrated landscape project solutions across design, build, and maintenance.

Design

Landscape master planning, public space design, exterior architectural landscape design, planting and irrigation design, and 3D visualization and rendering.

Build & Execution

Complete landscape construction — hardscape and softscape installation, ground preparation, drainage construction, and full landscape infrastructure works.

Maintenance

Ongoing plant maintenance, irrigation system maintenance, landscape rehabilitation, and seasonal planting and care programs for lasting results.

In-House Fabrication

Metal workshop for mold fabrication, machine frames, and custom landscape structures. Carpentry workshop for casting molds, timber structures, and decking.

Transportation & Delivery

Own logistics fleet for professional on-time delivery of TEMACO products across Rwanda and beyond, with vehicle leasing available to external clients.

Materials Sourcing

Supply of complementary construction materials including laterite, aggregates, and sand — a one-stop solution for complete project requirements.

About TEMACO

Founded in 2013 by Emmanuel Tuyisenge, a passionate Chemistry graduate, TEMACO Builders Ltd has become Rwanda's leading manufacturer of high-quality concrete construction materials.

Our mission from inception has been to focus on the paving and cladding subsector, offering durable and aesthetic products manufactured in the most innovative way. We entered this market with almost no competition but maintained our commitment to excellence.

At the dawn of our ten-year anniversary, we celebrate not just our success but our positive impact on the community through job creation, youth training, and environmental consciousness.

Why We Exist

To raise the beauty of places to higher levels and protect our environment through quality concrete construction materials.

What We Believe

Our business is a medium for making positive impact, improving lives, and creating wealth for communities.

Our Vision

Expanding operations, capacitating more people, and being the regional leader in concrete products innovation.

Our Awards

Best Exhibitor "Made In Rwanda" Expo 2017, Best Young Entrepreneur for Job Creation 2018, and many more.
