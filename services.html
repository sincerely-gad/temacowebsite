Pillar 03 — Services

What We Do

From concept to completion — TEMACO delivers integrated landscape services across design, build, maintenance, in-house fabrication, and logistics.

Design Build & Execution Maintenance In-House Fabrication Transport & Sourcing
Service 01

Design

Landscape master planning, exterior architectural design, and full 3D visualisation services — transforming client visions into buildable, beautiful landscapes.

Landscape Master Planning & Design

TEMACO's in-house design team produces comprehensive landscape plans from initial concept through to construction-ready documentation. We work across residential estates, public parks, institutional campuses, and mixed-use developments.

  • Landscape master planning for public and private spaces
  • Exterior architectural landscape design
  • Planting plans and plant scheduling
  • Irrigation system design and hydraulic calculations
  • 3D visualisation and photorealistic rendering
  • Construction documentation and bill of quantities

Master Planning

Spatial layout and design strategy for large-scale landscapes integrating function, ecology, and aesthetics.

3D Visualisation

Photorealistic renderings and walkthrough animations that allow clients to experience the design before ground breaks.

Irrigation Design

Hydraulic calculations, pipe routing, and emitter selection for efficient, zone-controlled water delivery systems.

Technical Documentation

Full construction drawings, specifications, and bills of quantities ready for contractor tendering and project execution.

Service 02

Build & Project Execution

Complete landscape construction services — TEMACO manages end-to-end project delivery from site preparation through to final handover across hardscape and softscape scopes.

End-to-End Landscape Construction

Our site teams deliver the full spectrum of landscape construction works — from subgrade preparation and drainage through to final paving, planting, and irrigation commissioning. We self-perform the majority of works using our own trained workforce and equipment.

  • Site clearance and topographic preparation
  • Drainage and stormwater construction
  • Hardscape installation — paving, kerbs, walls, and structures
  • Softscape installation — planting, turfing, and hydroseeding
  • Irrigation system installation and commissioning
  • Project management and quality assurance

Site Preparation

Ground clearing, earthworks, subgrade compaction, and drainage installation as the foundation for all landscape works.

Hardscape Installation

Precision laying of paving, kerbs, cast stone, cladding, and all concrete landscape elements to specification.

Softscape Installation

Planting, turfing, hydroseeding, and mulching works by trained horticulturalists using nursery-grown plant stock.

Project Management

Dedicated project managers coordinating subcontractors, logistics, quality, and client reporting from start to handover.

Service 03

Landscape Maintenance

Ongoing grounds maintenance programmes to keep landscapes thriving — covering plant care, irrigation servicing, and landscape rehabilitation.

Grounds & Landscape Maintenance Programmes

TEMACO offers structured maintenance contracts for residential estates, commercial properties, public parks, and institutional campuses — ensuring planted landscapes remain healthy, safe, and visually outstanding throughout the year.

  • Regular mowing, edging, and lawn care
  • Pruning, shaping, and seasonal plant care
  • Irrigation system maintenance and seasonal adjustment
  • Plant replacement, gap filling, and replanting
  • Fertilisation, pest management, and soil health monitoring
  • Landscape rehabilitation and remedial works

Grounds Care

Scheduled lawn mowing, edging, hedge trimming, and general grounds maintenance to agreed service levels.

Irrigation Servicing

Routine inspection, cleaning, adjustment, and repair of irrigation systems to ensure efficient and effective water delivery.

Plant Health

Integrated pest and disease management, fertilisation programmes, and soil health assessments for healthy plant stock.

Rehabilitation Works

Remedial landscape works to restore neglected or storm-damaged landscapes back to full specification and functionality.

Service 04

In-House Fabrication

Dedicated metal and carpentry workshops producing custom landscape structures, casting moulds, machine frames, and bespoke architectural elements.

Images Coming Soon

Metal Workshop & Carpentry Workshop

TEMACO's on-site workshops give us the capability to fabricate bespoke items in-house — reducing lead times, controlling quality, and enabling unique design solutions that off-the-shelf products cannot deliver.

  • Metal mould fabrication for TEMACO precast products
  • Machine frames and custom brackets for production equipment
  • Custom landscape structures — pergolas, trellises, and gates
  • Carpentry moulds and formwork for concrete casting
  • Timber decking, boardwalks, and outdoor furniture
  • Bespoke architectural timber and metal features

Metal Workshop

Fabrication of cast moulds, machine components, gates, pergola structures, and custom metalwork for landscape projects.

Carpentry Workshop

Timber mould making, formwork, decking, boardwalks, and bespoke outdoor furniture produced to client specification.

Custom Design Fabrication

One-off and bespoke fabricated items designed in collaboration with clients and architects for unique landscape features.

Production Support

In-house fabrication support for TEMACO's precast manufacturing operations — keeping production efficient and flexible.

Service 05

Transport & Materials Sourcing

Own logistics fleet ensuring reliable, on-time delivery of TEMACO products and materials, plus a one-stop sourcing solution for complementary construction materials.

Integrated Logistics & Material Supply

TEMACO operates its own fleet of delivery vehicles capable of transporting bulk materials, precast products, and equipment to sites across Rwanda and into neighbouring countries. Combined with our in-house materials sourcing capability, we offer clients a genuinely consolidated supply chain.

  • Professional delivery of TEMACO products across Rwanda
  • Cross-border logistics into Uganda, DRC, and Burundi
  • Vehicle leasing to external clients and contractors
  • Supply of laterite, aggregates, and construction sand
  • Bulk materials sourcing and project procurement support
  • Scheduled delivery programmes for long-term site supply

Product Delivery

Scheduled and on-demand delivery of all TEMACO manufactured products to construction sites nationwide.

Regional Logistics

Cross-border transportation into Uganda, DRC, Burundi, and Tanzania — extending TEMACO's supply reach across the region.

Materials Sourcing

Supply of laterite, aggregate, sand, and complementary construction materials — a complete one-stop project supply solution.

Vehicle Leasing

Leasing of trucks and transport vehicles to external contractors, project managers, and organisations requiring reliable logistics.
